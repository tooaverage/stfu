# Japan page copy

## Hero
Title: Go late March to May, or November.
Sub: Spring brings cherry blossom and dry, mild days. November brings red maples and clear skies. Both are comfortable for long days of walking.

## Weather section
Title: What the weather does all year
Intro: Figures are for Tokyo, which is close to Kyoto and Osaka. Hokkaido runs about 8°C colder and Okinawa about 7°C warmer.

## Month notes
January: Cold, dry and sunny. Clear Fuji views and quiet sights after the New Year rush. Peak ski season in Hokkaido.
February: Still cold and dry. Plum blossom starts late in the month. Sapporo Snow Festival draws crowds in early February.
March: Warming fast. Cherry blossom opens in Tokyo and Kyoto around the 22nd. The last week is when the best window starts.
April: Peak blossom in the first week, then fresh green and mild days. Book early; Golden Week begins on the 29th.

## Cities section
Title: Best time for each city
Intro: Japan is 3,000 km long, so the timing shifts. Hokkaido is at its best in summer, when Honshu is too hot. Okinawa is good in early spring and late autumn but sits in the typhoon path from July to September.

## Seasons section
Title: Cherry blossom, autumn leaves, snow and quiet
Intro: Blossom moves north through spring and the leaves move south through autumn. Forecasts are published from January (sakura) and September (koyo), so set your exact dates once they are out.

## Route section
Title: Tokyo to Osaka in two weeks
Intro: Fly into Tokyo and out of Osaka (Kansai airport) so you never backtrack. Every leg is a direct shinkansen ride. Send big bags ahead with a luggage forwarding service (takkyubin) for about ¥2,500 each.

## Shinkansen section
Title: How to book a shinkansen ticket
Intro: The bullet train is easier than it looks. On the Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka–Hiroshima line there are three kinds of train. All of them use the same tracks and the same tickets; only the number of stops changes.
